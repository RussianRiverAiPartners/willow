Willow's Health Tracker: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Willow's Health Tracker ("Willow" or "the app") is a personal tracker for thyroid health, cycles, symptoms and food. This policy explains what happens to your information.

Your entries stay on your phone

Everything you log in Willow, including check-ins, symptoms, period days, lab results, notes and your shopping list, is stored only on your device. We do not have accounts, we do not run a server that receives your entries, and we cannot see them.

What leaves your phone

  • Food lookups. When you scan a barcode or search for a food, the barcode number or search words are sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), a public food database, to fetch the product's ingredients. No health entries are sent. Open Food Facts may see your device's internet address, as any website does.
  • Things you choose to share. If you tap Share on your doctor summary or shopping list, it goes only where you send it.
  • Feedback. If you send us feedback by email or through TestFlight, we receive what you write and any screenshot you attach.

Camera and notifications

The camera is used only to read barcodes. Images are processed on your device and are not saved or sent. Reminders are scheduled on your device.

No ads, no tracking, no selling

Willow has no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools. We do not sell or share personal information.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your phone deletes everything you logged. If your phone backs up to iCloud, your backup is governed by Apple's terms.

Children

Willow is not directed to children under 13.

Not medical advice

Willow helps you keep track. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and food results can be wrong. Always read the label and ask your doctor about medical questions.

Changes and contact
