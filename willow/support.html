Willow's Health Tracker: Support

Need help or have an idea? Email willowtracker.support@gmail.com. We read everything.

Common questions

Where is my data? Only on your phone. See the privacy policy.

How does my flower grow? It grows a little each time you check in during the month. It droops after a few days away and perks up when you return. A healthy flower on the last day of the month is yours to keep in your garden.

A food result looks wrong. Product data comes from a public database and can be incomplete. Always read the label.

Is Willow medical advice? No. It is a diary to help you and your doctor see patterns.
